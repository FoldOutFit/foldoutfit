Shelf 01 The Basics

A bag that unfolds
into a closet.

  • Three hanging shelves
  • Hangs on a stall or locker hook that’s already there
  • Keeps your clothes, shoes, and gear off the floor while you change

The problem

The balancing act.

Public restrooms and locker rooms almost never give you a clean surface to change on. So everyone improvises.

  1. 01

    Awkwardly holding clean clothes

    Keeping clean clothing separate and managed is a struggle with nowhere to put it.

  2. 02

    Balancing on one foot

    Avoiding dirty surfaces forces uncomfortable postures every time you change.

  3. 03

    Bags that don’t help

    Standard bags get messy fast, mixing clean clothes in with dirty items.

Who it’s for

Anyone who changes away from home.

  • Students & athletes

    Swimmers, runners, football players and theater kids who change in locker rooms and restrooms and need something fast and hygienic.

  • Gym-goers

    Before work, after work, or between classes. Change quickly without setting your clean clothes on a wet bench or locker-room floor.

  • Travelers

    Commuters, truck drivers and frequent travelers who pay for showers on the road and need a clean place to change.

Shelf 02 The Details

Hang it, let it drop open, and change.

Zipped up, it is about 16 by 11.5 inches, so it carries like a regular bag to practice, the gym, or a commute.

  1. Step 1

    Carry it closed, like any other bag.

  2. Step 2

    Hang it on a stall or locker hook and let the shelves fold down.

  3. Step 3

    Change. Each shelf holds something, off the floor. Then zip it back up.

How it’s built

Built to hang and hold its shape.

It has a stable back and a hinge at the top, so it hangs and holds its shape. It hooks on a standard stall hanger.

Closed size
About 16 × 11.5 in
Hangs on
A standard stall or locker hook
Shelves
Three, for folded clothes
Hygiene First
A removable insert at the bottom, so socks are not sitting on the tile
Shoes
A separate spot, away from clean clothes
Hinge
3D-printed, so the fabric middle section can fold
Logo
Fold-Out-Fit embroidered on the front. For a school or team order, your logo can be embroidered too, at no extra charge.
