The bag

A bag that unfolds into a closet.

  • Three hanging shelves
  • Hangs on a stall or locker hook that’s already there
  • Keeps your clothes, shoes, and gear off the floor while you change
  • Made for public restrooms and locker rooms, where there’s almost never a clean place to put your things
Shelf 2

How it works

Hang it, let it drop open, and change.

Zipped up, it is about 16 by 11.5 inches, so it carries like a regular bag to practice, the gym, or a commute.

  1. 1Carry it closed, like any other bag.
  2. 2Hang it on a stall or locker hook and let the shelves fold down.
  3. 3Change. Each shelf holds something, off the floor. Then zip it back up.

It has a stable back and a hinge at the top, so it hangs and holds its shape. It hooks on a standard stall hanger. There are three shelves for folded clothes, a removable “Hygiene First” insert at the bottom so socks are not sitting on the tile, a separate spot for shoes, and a 3D-printed hinge that lets the fabric middle section fold. The Fold-Out-Fit logo is embroidered on the front. For a school or team order, your logo can be embroidered too, at no extra charge.
