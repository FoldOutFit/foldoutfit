The solution

Turn any stall into a closet.

Fold-Out-Fit hooks onto a standard stall hanger and drops open into stacked shelves, using vertical space you already have. Folded clothes up top, shoes kept separate at the bottom, nothing on the tile.

Universal compatibility

Hooks onto standard stall hangers for quick, secure installation.

Removable insert

The "Hygiene First" bottom insert keeps socks from touching restroom tiles.

Wrinkle-free storage

A shelf-like structure built for folded clothes keeps creases to a minimum.

Innovative hinge

Dual compartments

A dedicated section keeps shoes separate from your garments.

How it works

Hang. Unfold. Change.

STEP 01

Carry it like a bag

Zipped up, it's a compact carry bag that goes to practice, the gym or your commute.

STEP 02

Hook and drop

Hang it on any stall or locker hook and let the shelves fold down into place.

STEP 03

Change clean

Everything has its own shelf off the floor. When you're done, it folds back up.

Who it's for

Anyone who changes away from home.

Students & athletes

Swimmers, runners, football players and theater kids who change in locker rooms and restrooms and need something fast and hygienic.

Gym-goers

Before work, after work, or between classes. Change quickly without setting your clean clothes on a wet bench or locker-room floor.

Travelers

Commuters, truck drivers and frequent travelers who pay for showers on the road and need a clean place to change.
