The bag

About 16 by 11.5 inches when it’s zipped up. Hang it, let it drop open, and change. When you’re done, it folds back up.

What it includes:

  • A stable back and a hinge at the top, so it hangs and holds its shape.
  • It hooks on a standard stall hanger.
  • Three shelves for folded clothes.
  • A removable “Hygiene First” insert at the bottom, so socks aren’t sitting on the tile.
  • A separate spot for shoes.
  • A 3D-printed hinge that lets the fabric middle section fold.
  • The Fold-Out-Fit logo embroidered on the front. For a school or team order, your logo can be embroidered too, at no extra charge.

It’s for anyone who changes away from home: students and athletes, people at the gym, and travelers who use showers on the road.
