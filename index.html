The brief

The balancing act nobody asked for.

Public restrooms and locker rooms almost never give you a clean surface to change on. So everyone improvises: clean clothes bunched in one hand, a bag sliding onto a wet bench, a one-footed dance to keep socks off the tile.

“So everyone improvises.”

Standard bags don’t help much either. They get messy fast, mixing what you just wore with what you meant to keep clean. Fold-Out-Fit was designed for that exact moment — a closet you can hang on the hook that’s already there.

How it works

Three motions.

First

Carry it like a bag

Zipped up, it’s a compact carry bag that goes to practice, the gym or your commute.

Then

Hook and drop

Hang it on any stall or locker hook and let the shelves fold down into place.

After that

Change clean

Everything has its own shelf off the floor. When you’re done, it folds back up.

Who it’s for

Anyone who changes away from home.

Students & athletes

Swimmers, runners, football players and theater kids who change in locker rooms and restrooms and need something fast and hygienic.

Gym-goers

Before work, after work, or between classes. Change quickly without setting your clean clothes on a wet bench or locker-room floor.

Travelers

Commuters, truck drivers and frequent travelers who pay for showers on the road and need a clean place to change.

Inside the design

What it’s built with.

  • A stable back and hinge

    A stable back support and a reliable top hinge make it durable and easy to use.

  • A hook you already have

    Hooks onto standard stall hangers for quick, secure installation.

  • The Hygiene First insert

    The removable “Hygiene First” bottom insert keeps socks from touching restroom tiles.

  • Shelves for folded clothes

    A shelf-like structure built for folded clothes keeps creases to a minimum.

  • A 3D-printed hinge

    A unique 3D-printed hinge system allows a flexible, fabric-based middle section.

  • A place for shoes

    A dedicated section keeps shoes separate from your garments.
