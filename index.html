I made this because I was tired of changing in a stall with my stuff on the floor.

I’m Warren. Fold-Out-Fit is a bag I built that hooks on a regular stall or locker hook and unfolds into three shelves. Your clothes, shoes, and gear stay off the floor while you change. When you’re done, it folds back up.

How it works

Zipped up, it’s about 16 by 11.5 inches, so it carries like a regular bag to practice, the gym, or a commute.

  1. 1.Carry it closed, like any other bag.
  2. 2.Hang it on a stall or locker hook and let the shelves fold down.
  3. 3.Change. Each shelf holds something, off the floor. Then zip it back up.

Here it is unfolding, if you want to see the motion.

What’s on it

A few things I cared about while I was building it:

  • A stable back and a hinge at the top, so it hangs and holds its shape.
  • It hooks on a standard stall hanger.
  • Three shelves for folded clothes, so things stay neater than they would in a heap.
  • A removable “Hygiene First” insert at the bottom, so socks aren’t sitting on the tile.
  • A separate spot for shoes.
  • A 3D-printed hinge that lets the fabric middle section fold.
  • The Fold-Out-Fit logo embroidered on the front. For a school or team order, I can embroider your logo too, at no extra charge.

I built it for anyone who changes away from home. Students and athletes (swimmers, runners, football, theater), people who go to the gym before or after work, and travelers who use showers on the road.
